# BBMST overlap-pattern strategy

Research design only; not a completed theorem.

Keep the proof in three auditable layers: incidence, exact mass, and stagewise distortion. For a finite product space with factorized point weights and normalized coordinate weights, derive the mass of a cylinder fixing coordinates F to values v_i as the product of the fixed-coordinate weights. Do not postulate the mass formula.

For two cylinders, disagreement on a shared fixed coordinate makes the intersection empty. If they agree, merge the prescriptions on the union of fixed-coordinate sets and count each shared coordinate once. For Z(x)=sum_j 1_{H_j}(x), prove E[Z^2]=sum_j mu(H_j)+sum_{j != k} mu(H_j intersect H_k) by finite-sum algebra. Group ordered pairs by overlap pattern; do not replace intersection mass by a product unless independence is separately established.

The next formal lemma should be a generic finite product-sum identity with coordinatewise delta functions. Instantiate it for cylinder mass, then compatible intersection mass, then the second moment. The remaining hard part is the source-faithful quantitative distortion estimate, not the finite algebra.

Literature: Balister et al., Acta Math. Hungar. 161 (2020), DOI 10.1007/s10474-020-01048-z; Balister et al., Invent. Math. 228 (2022), DOI 10.1007/s00222-021-01087-5.
