# JARVIS International IP and Legal Release Register
Status: DRAFT — not legally approved, not a patent or trademark filing. 2026-11-23.

## IP ownership
Record original authors, dates, commits, contracts and assignments to the actual operating entity. GitHub control is not proof of copyright title.
Create a dependency SBOM and review every package, AI model and asset for license, attribution, source-distribution and commercial/royalty obligations. No invented royalty percentages.
Trademark name and logo: clearance and registration UNVERIFIED. Do not claim registered status.
Patent candidates: confidential invention disclosure, inventorship, novelty/prior-art search, technical contribution, territory, attorney review and filing. Do not claim a patent has been filed or granted.

## Customer-facing legal documents — drafts requiring counsel review
Terms: B2C/B2B, offer versus binding order, prices/taxes, payment, performance, limitations subject to mandatory law, governing law, dispute handling.
Privacy: verified controller identity, contacts, legal bases, data categories, processors, international transfers, retention, user rights, complaint mechanism and recommendation/profiling explanation.
Imprint: verified company identity, address, representatives, registration and tax details as applicable; no placeholders presented as facts.
Withdrawal: mandatory consumer information and accessible online withdrawal functionality in relevant jurisdictions.
Third-party licenses: complete notices, copyright attribution, required license texts and source offers where applicable.
Accessibility and cookie/marketing controls: audit, separate optional consent, revocation and audit logs.

## International rules
Determine rules by customer country, customer type and service/product, not merely interface language. Preserve mandatory local consumer rights.
Before enabling paid checkout in each market, check consumer, privacy, tax, product safety/import, payment, accessibility and dispute obligations.
Store accepted legal-document versions and timestamps. Display material terms clearly before order; footer text may be compact but must remain legible and accessible.
EU online withdrawal function requirements apply from June 2026 for covered distance contracts.

## Release gates
1. Verify entity and IP assignments.
2. Audit all third-party and AI licenses.
3. Clear trademarks and assess patent candidates with counsel.
4. Obtain locally reviewed legal texts and verified legal identity.
5. Test consumer checkout, privacy controls, withdrawal, security and accessibility.
6. Record evidence and sign-off per market.
