# JARVIS — proposed founder-owned IP structure
Status: founder instruction recorded as proposed allocation; NOT an executed assignment, license or legal opinion.
Date: 2026-11-23

## Founder intention
The individual founder intends to retain personally all intellectual property rights that the founder actually owns or lawfully acquires in original JARVIS software, original visual assets, technical inventions and protectable know-how.
The Marshall Islands LLC is intended to be a separately identified operating/coordination entity and potential licensee, not an automatic IP assignee.
The founder's German sole-proprietor activity and the LLC must each be identified by actual legal status and transaction role. Customer-facing contracting party must be disclosed before any binding transaction.
Autonomous sourcing and outreach may operate independently of the customer portal, subject to applicable laws, permission, budget limits, human approvals and accurate revenue reporting.

## Required founder-to-LLC license (not yet signed)
Written non-exclusive, revocable or terminable, limited-purpose license; territories, sublicensing, modifications, derivative works, confidentiality, source-code access, permitted brands, liability, governing law, fees or royalty-free treatment, related-party valuation, and termination consequences to be reviewed.
No assignment of founder-owned IP is inferred from repository access, deployment, LLC membership or this memo.
Third-party software, trademarks, patent rights and model weights remain subject to their actual licensors.

## Separate commercial and tax treatment
Identify the contracting seller/provider on every order, quote and invoice; assign payments to the actual contracting entity.
Verify German trade registration, tax obligations and whether activity is a trade or profession; do not assume incorporation may wait until substantial profits.
Determine whether German management/control of the LLC creates German tax or registration consequences, and review related-party licensing and cross-border tax.
No earnings or profits are established without reconciled paid invoices and costs.
