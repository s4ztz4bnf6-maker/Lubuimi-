# SGL Global Logistics Network (SGL-GLN)
Algorithm: SGL-GLOA. Worldwide, no country preference. Candidate providers only, not contracted: DHL, UPS, FedEx, Aramex, EMS, USPS, Canada Post, Correios, Servientrega, DPD, GLS, Hermes, SPL, Emirates Post, SF Express, Japan Post, India Post, Australia Post, NZ Post, national postal operators and authorized local couriers.
Adapters must obtain current all-in quotes, shipment dimensions, service area, customs eligibility, duties/taxes, insurance, handoff compatibility, time windows and currency conversion evidence. Unknown costs or legal eligibility block booking. Provider registry should track coverage, API permissions, rate limits, quote provenance, expiry and reliability. No bypass of sanctions, customs or provider rules. No advance spending; settled customer funds and owner approval required. Free item + actual service costs + 10% handling; statutory taxes disclosed as applicable.
Synthetic test scenarios (not live quotes): USA to Kenya; Saudi Arabia to Timbuktu, Mali; Morocco to Nunavut, Canada; Japan to Brazil; Australia to Senegal. Test direct versus multi-leg, disconnected destinations, expired quotes, incompatible handoffs, customs blocks, and currency mismatch. No fabricated availability.
Current optimizer enumerates simple eligible time-compatible routes up to a configurable leg limit and minimizes quoted costs in one currency. All outputs are non-bookable until end-to-end validation. It does not yet implement live adapters, cross-currency conversion, consolidation or customs verification. Marketplace remains future-only.
