# Second Good Life — My Treasure, Your Pleasure
Replaces the Freegoods concept. Commercial, transparent reuse brokerage. Free item; actual third-party service costs plus exactly 10% handling, rounded to currency minor units. No hidden markup or JARVIS advance spending. Existing owner spending approval remains required.
## Workflow
Authorized listing feeds -> active free offers and wanted listings -> candidate matching -> human/party confirmation -> verified reservation -> suitable logistics quotes -> cheapest verified total -> transparent quote -> consent and settled funds -> approved booking -> tracking and receipt. No unsolicited bulk messaging, no fabricated matches, no scraping against platform rules. Store minimal personal data.

## Logistics
Compare direct pickup, parcel, courier and consolidated routes using verified all-in quotes; reject unsuitable, expired or unverified quotes. Future multi-leg optimization must include customs, packaging, insurance and delivery constraints. No guarantee of global coverage.
