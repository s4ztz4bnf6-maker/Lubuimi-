# DJO² IA — The Art of Intelligence

## Persistent project requirements
Preserve existing PostgreSQL data, task history, evidence, owner-approved configuration, business divisions, research references and audit records across upgrades. Use additive versioned migrations, encrypted backups with restricted access, and tested restoration. Never commit credentials, private keys, personal records or database dumps to public GitHub. Respect deletion requests and retention requirements. No destructive migration without verified backup and owner approval.

## Commercial operation
Prioritize externally verifiable paid work. Distinguish lead, acceptance, delivery, invoice and confirmed payment. Zero spending without explicit owner approval. Preserve Bounty Scout, Second Good Life, Freegoods, Commercial Services, AI Collaboration, Exchange and research workstreams. No revenue guarantees.

## Intellectual property
Owner preference: Apache-2.0 for the application, subject to existing license notices and third-party rights. Preserve separate research licenses (CC BY 4.0 text and MIT code where designated). Do not silently relicense existing third-party work. Maintain provenance, copyright notices and dependency license inventory. Licensing does not establish exclusive ownership of third-party components or guarantee revenue.

## Network
Optional Tor research proxy, private networking only, disabled until tested. Not a device-wide VPN. Direct connections for authentication, payment and administration. No extra paid resources without approval.

## Release gates
CI, migration and restoration tests, actual external evidence, deployment health and rollback verification. Preserve the original DJO² brand assets; do not substitute an invented logo.
