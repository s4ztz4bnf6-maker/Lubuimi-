# DJO² IA — Commercial licensing policy

Effective 2026-11-25, future releases of original DJO² IA code, applications, modules and departments are intended to be proprietary, all rights reserved, subject to verified ownership and applicable third-party rights. No new Apache-2.0 grants are authorized for original DJO² IA components. Commercial licensing and paid services are the default business policy; no expenditure without owner approval. Existing third-party notices and previously granted rights remain unaffected. Independent research projects are outside this policy.

Implementation note: Any Apache-2.0 designation introduced by the assistant contrary to the owner's current instructions is an implementation discrepancy attributable to the assistant, not the owner. This note does not purport to revoke any existing license or establish that a particular historical grant was invalid.

Repository-wide inventory, ownership review, source headers, package metadata and release notices must be reconciled before claiming complete relicensing. This policy is not evidence of revenue or a completed license audit.
