# Exact sample applications of the one-prime CRT lifting criterion

Date: 2026-10-30

This note records exact arithmetic instances of the criterion in
research/GENERAL_ONE_PRIME_CRT_LIFTING_CRITERION_2026-10-30.md.
The table is illustrative, not an exhaustive classification.

For each odd base M, let D(M) be all divisors d>1, tau=τ(M), and q=tau-1. The hypotheses are:
- M(1-Σ_{d in D(M)}1/d)>1;
- for every p^a exactly dividing M, tau/(a+1)>=3;
- q is prime and q does not divide M.

Then no distinct-modulus covering system can have all its moduli divide Mq.

| 315 | 3²·5·7 | 12 | 11 | 103/105 | 6 |
| 675 | 3³·5² | 12 | 11 | 113/135 | 110 |

| 36465 | 3·5·11·13·17 | 32 | 31 | 12037/12155 | 354 |

All entries are exact rational/integer calculations. For each row the exponent condition is checked prime by prime:
- M=165: tau/(a+1)=4 for each of its three primes;
- M=315: values 4,6,6;
- M=675: values 3,4;
- M=3675: values 9,6,6;
- M=9009: values 8,12,12,12;
- M=36465: values 16 for each prime.

These examples show that the criterion is not peculiar to 315. However, they do not approach the global conjecture by themselves: each excludes only one explicitly structured LCM Mq, and some are subsumed by stronger external low-rank or lcm lower bounds.
