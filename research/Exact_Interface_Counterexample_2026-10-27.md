# Exact BBMST moment-interface counterexample

Date: 2026-10-27
Status: rigorous counterexample to the abstract interface, not to the original theorem.

## Counterexample
For each integer n≥2, take n equally weighted fibres pᵢ=1/n and αᵢ=1/n. Then pᵢ≥0, Σpᵢ=1 and 0≤αᵢ≤1, but
  Γₙ = Σᵢ pᵢ αᵢ(1−αᵢ) = (1/n)(1−1/n) → 0.

Therefore normalization and interval constraints alone cannot imply Γ≥δ for any universal δ>0. Nor can they imply a uniform positive mass of fibres bounded away from both endpoints. This is an exact rational family, not a numerical search.

This is not a counterexample to the original BBMST theorem: the family has not been shown to satisfy the source's geometric/hyperplane constraints. It proves that those constraints must be used essentially.

## Consequence
Do not pursue a moment-gap theorem from normalized weights and 0≤α≤1 alone. The next target must be a source-faithful geometric inequality derived from the original construction.
