## Verification and provenance

A mathematical claim, a Lean kernel result, and a commit-specific CI result are distinct. Any formal-verification claim must identify the exact source commit, declaration scope, Lean/Mathlib toolchain, printed axiom profile, sorryAx scan, and the relevant successful CI logs. A failed later integration run does not erase an earlier successful result on its own exact commit; a successful compile does not establish a theorem beyond the declarations actually checked.
